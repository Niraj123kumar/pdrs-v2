What is this?

PDRS helps you rehearse defending your project. Students submit their project details and go through sessions of AI-generated reviewer questions with scored feedback. Faculty run live panel sessions with annotations.

Phase 1 boots the server, database, and static frontend. Feature flows land in later phases.
